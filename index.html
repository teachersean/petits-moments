About this proof of concept & your privacy

This is a phrase companion, not a full language course or pronunciation assessor. Your saved phrases stay in this browser’s local storage, not in an account. They do not sync to other devices and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Don’t enter private family information.

Listen uses your device/browser’s speech voices. French or Korean voices may not be installed; sound and offline availability vary. Your browser or operating system may use an online voice service. Nothing is recorded.

Custom phrases are not translated or checked automatically. Use short phrases you have verified.
